This is a decision log, not a scientific result ledger. Frozen evidence remains
in its original records. New entries should give the decision, rationale, scope,
reversibility and related package/PR without exposing private information.

## 2026-10-24 - Autonomous implementation authority

The owner explicitly requested a logical continuation plan executed by autonomous
AI agents, with human approval/opinions requested only for safety or actual money
use. Ordinary planning, technical choices, AI review, tested merges and scoped
zero-cost engineering releases therefore have standing authorization. Higher-priority
permissions remain binding. Actual incremental spending defaults to EUR 0; another
project's budget does not apply. See [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

This changes workflow approval, not mathematical, security or evidence standards.
Missing scientific evidence cannot be replaced with either human or AI confidence.

remain outside scope because of their safety/reputational implications.
