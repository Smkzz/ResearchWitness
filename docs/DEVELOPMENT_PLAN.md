## Objective and success

Build a usable source-to-evidence system that discovers more defensible research
discrepancies at a controlled false-alarm rate and cost than frozen baselines.
Start with supported numerical relationships and bounded mathematical claims.
Do not promise to certify an arbitrary paper, prove an empirical law from text,
or establish a breakthrough before a comparative evaluation supports it.

The first deliverable is one coherent installed audit workflow, not another
parallel prototype. The research deliverable is a reproducible benchmark and a
measured answer to whether bounded adversarial discovery plus independent
verification improves on simpler methods. A well-supported negative result is
valid research; relabeling it as success is not.

Reuse these implementations where sound. Do not rebuild their functions behind

### RW-04F — statistical power feasibility without fabricated evidence

The bounded [hypothetical power planner](POWER_FEASIBILITY.md) takes
source-free paired-positive discordance assumptions and computes the exact
chance of meeting RW-04E's one-sided positive test **and** practical gain,
plus the minimum *zero/zero-alarm* negative-family size required by the
declared conservative safety bound. It does not compute safety-test power
from incomplete marginal assumptions. Exemplary 129 positive plus 86
negative families are not a qualified real enrolment target; at 98% invented
joint silence, 86 negative families have only about 17.6% chance of no
alarms. A real scientific study needs justified development-exposed effect
and false-alarm distributions, a complete paired negative harm power model,
selection/exclusion accounting and an independently pre-outcome frozen
protocol. RW-01O's first 2–5 real families are pilot-only. Do not lower
RW-03/RW-05 custody gates or claim a groundbreaking scientific result
without independently grounded real outcomes.

### RW-04G — full paired negative harm power (hypothetical only)

The new [joint family-power analysis](JOINT_POWER_FEASIBILITY.md) removes
the earlier zero/zero-only shortcut by enumerating all four pairwise
negative-arm alarm outcomes under **source-free fictional** probabilities.
Every candidate/baseline alarm count pair is judged by the exact RW-04E
Bonferroni Clopper-Pearson harm criterion. Combined study success uses
the primary and negative safety probabilities **only** under explicit
hypothetical independence of positive and negative work families.
With the example's 129 positive + 86 negative families and stated
invented rates, 80.28% primary success is compatible with only 33.85%
chance of passing both endpoints. This overturns any claim that 215
families inherently supplies an 80%-powered confirmatory study.
Actual rate estimation, family-correlation and missingness sensitivity,
privacy-preserving source adjudication and a genuine pre-outcome
registration remain separate gates; the first authorized 2–5-source
development pilot [RW-01O](https://github.com/Smkzz/ResearchWitness/issues/35)
remains unperformed. No public report should call this a breakthrough
in scientific error discovery.

### RW-04H — joint event-dependence and missing-family sensitivity

[RW-04H](STUDY_SENSITIVITY.md) provides a source-free, exact rational and
bounded hypothetical utility to expose the earlier RW-04G **cross-endpoint
independence** premise. Given only marginal endpoint pass chances, it returns
sharp Fréchet joint bounds. Separately, its exact binomial quota planner
assumes independent retention **within** each positive/negative stratum and
allocates the joint quota failure budget by union bound, requiring no
independence **between** the strata. It does **not** multiply quota
completion with endpoint power, estimate missingness from real studies or
repair the original PMC evidence/custody gaps. The 129+86 complete-family
example under 10% *invented* loss requires 152+102 enrolled to give a
95%-or-better conservative joint-quota completion bound. These are
planning mathematics, **not** measured ResearchWitness superiority.

## Evidence, review and independence

AI agents can implement, review and adjudicate source-supported arithmetic without
routine human approval. They cannot self-declare data independence, fabricate source
provenance, treat shared-context consensus as ground truth, or declare empirical
truth from a formal certificate. Review modality and residual dependencies must be
reported. For claims needing unavailable domain/experimental evidence, preserve
`unresolved`; this is a scientific limitation, not a request for an opinion.

## Operating rules and maintenance

The execution ledger is the current work queue; this document defines its scope.
A package receipt records input/output identities, exact code/tree, real checks,
review modality, resource use, limitations and next action. The coordinator updates
status after each merged slice and records meaningful decisions in
[DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). Do not edit frozen scientific evidence to make current
status appear green. Changes to sampling, metrics or scope after exposure require
a new named protocol and explicit linkage to the old one.

No automatic scheduler is installed by this documentation. Each invoked agent
continues autonomously within its session and leaves resumable evidence on exit.
