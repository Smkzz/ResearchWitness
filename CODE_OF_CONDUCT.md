# Code of Conduct

ResearchWitness welcomes technical disagreement, adversarial review, and attempts to falsify claims. Those are part of the project's purpose. They must remain focused on evidence and software, not people.

## Expected conduct

- Be precise, respectful, and willing to revise a claim when evidence changes.
- Critique code, formalizations, assumptions, and evidence rather than researchers or contributors.
- Distinguish technical error from misconduct; do not infer intent from a discrepancy.
- Do not publish private manuscripts, correspondence, credentials, or personal information.
- Prefer reproducible examples over rhetoric.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deliberate misrepresentation of evidence, or using this project to organize targeted harassment of researchers or contributors is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

For ordinary project disagreements, use the issue or pull-request thread and keep discussion technical. For conduct problems involving personal or sensitive information, do not post those details publicly; contact the repository owner through the contact methods available on their GitHub profile. GitHub platform abuse can also be reported through GitHub's own reporting mechanisms.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its contributors.
